# Advise Insurance

> Advise Insurance is the Medicare distribution business of Advise Health Holdings, a
> Bloomington, Indiana company founded in 2018 by Sherman Rogers. Its operating brand is
> Bloom (bloomthrives.com), the Medicare telesales, quoting, enrollment and
> health-activation business Rogers launched in 2007. Bloom sells the Ascend platform
> (Ascend Quote & Enroll, Ascend Broker, Health Activations) to national and regional
> health plans and distributors on a white-label basis.

**This company publishes no API.** As of 2026-09-09 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no SDK, no webhook catalog, no MCP server, no agent card and no
machine-readable contract of any kind (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, WSDL, Protobuf or
Postman collection) anywhere on its public surface. If you are an agent looking for a way
to call Bloom programmatically, there is nothing to call — the integration motion runs
through the sales team at https://www.bloomthrives.com/contact-us/.

## The dead domain

`adviseinsurance.com` no longer serves a site. Every path on it — including paths that
cannot exist — returns HTTP 200 with the same 81,378-byte body, because the domain is
rewritten to `https://www.bloomthrives.com/wp-signup.php?new=adviseinsurance.com`, the
WordPress multisite "this site does not exist" shell. Treat every 200 from that host as a
soft 404. The domain is still registered (created 2017-07-27, Cloudflare, expires
2027-07-27), so it is parked by the company rather than lapsed.

## Domain security posture (probed 2026-09-09)

- www.bloomthrives.com: HTTPS, TLS 1.3, HSTS present, certificate valid to 2026-11-26.
- bloomthrives.com: SPF present (`-all`, Outlook + five IPv4 mechanisms; note the first
  mechanism is spelled `Ip4:` with a capital I). DMARC present at
  `_dmarc.bloomthrives.com` with `p=quarantine; pct=100`; a second, misplaced
  `v=DMARC1; p=reject` record sits at the apex where receivers do not read it. No DNSSEC.
  No CAA records.
